About this shelf

The shelf is kept by one person with a simple rule: only keep what is real, useful, and honestly described.

Books get annotations, not summaries. Tools get tested before they are recommended. Nothing is padded to fill a page, and nothing is written to trick a search engine into ranking it. Good content is content a machine can cite and a human can trust; those two goals agree far more often than they fight.

Some pages link to stores. If you buy through one, the shelf may earn a small commission. It never changes what is written or what is recommended, and it never will.

Kept by hand. Updated when there is something worth keeping.
