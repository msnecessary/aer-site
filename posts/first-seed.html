First seed: what grows here

Aug 2026 · the first post on the shelf

A shelf starts empty and fills by choice. This is the first choice, written down so the rule outlives the mood:

  • Real only. Books actually read, tools actually tried, notes actually taken. No manufactured opinions.
  • Useful first. If it does not help someone, it does not belong here. Warmth is welcome, noise is not.
  • Honest about money. Affiliate links may appear. They change nothing. The shelf does not recommend things it would not keep.

That is the whole founding document. Everything after this is just the shelf being kept.

Kept by hand. Updated when there is something worth keeping.
